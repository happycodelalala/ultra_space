# The heavy frame (builder validation). Run with ULTRA_SPACE_START_ALTITUDE=1500. Step out,
# choose the heavy frame (Shift+G: rover -> heavy frame), spawn it ahead (G), look up at it, walk
# up and board it, then open the builder around it for an overall view. Look for: a ~20 m
# humanoid frame standing on its feet (not sunk or floating) with four binders in an X on its
# back; the log lines "G now spawns: Heavy Frame", "a Heavy Frame appeared", "boarded the Heavy
# Frame", "building on the Heavy Frame (35 parts)"; no balance problems in the panel.
0.5  press Z
1    wait landed 20
2    press F                 # step out
3    hold Shift 0.3
3.1  press G                 # choose the heavy frame
4    press G                 # spawn it ahead
6    mouse 0 -12 0.5         # look up at it
7    shot 01-standing.png
7.5  mouse 0 12 0.5          # level again
8.5  hold W 2.5              # walk up to its feet
11.5 press F                 # board it
13.5 shot 02-chase.png
14   press B                 # builder: orbit view of the whole frame
16   shot 03-builder.png
16.5 hold MouseRight 1.5     # around to its back (the binders)
16.5 mouse 12 0 1.5
18.5 shot 04-binders.png
19   press B
20   quit
