# The Bastion (an original heavy frame with four manipulator arms and a closing shell). Run with
# ULTRA_SPACE_START_ALTITUDE=1500. Spawn it, board it, step through its poses with P (name
# order: arms_deployed, arms_grab, arms_stowed, shell_closed, shell_open, stand), then walk a
# little with the shell open and stop. Look for: four arms spreading wide with open clamps,
# reaching forward with closed clamps, folding back; the six slabs hanging straight down into a
# six-sided shell, then swinging far out; walking and standing after; log "pose: ..." lines.
0.5  press Z
1    wait landed 40
2    press F
3    hold Shift 0.3
3.1  press G                 # rover -> Bastion
4    press G                 # spawn it ahead
5    hold W 4                # walk up to it
9.5  press F                 # board it
11   press H                 # hide the HUD
12   shot 01-rest.png
12.5 press P                 # arms deployed
16   shot 02-arms-deployed.png
16.5 press P                 # arms grab
20   shot 03-arms-grab.png
20.5 press P                 # arms stowed
24   shot 04-arms-stowed.png
24.5 press P                 # shell closed (arms stowed)
28.5 shot 05-shell-closed.png
29   press P                 # shell open
33   shot 06-shell-open.png
33.5 press M                 # walk
34   hold W 6
38   shot 07-walking.png
40.5 press M                 # stop on both feet
45   shot 08-stopped.png
45.5 quit
