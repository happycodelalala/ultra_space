# Rover (task 3.5). Run with ULTRA_SPACE_START_ALTITUDE=1500 (below the start spot's ground, so the
# ship is placed on the surface; Z cancels its orbital speed). Step out, spawn a rover (G), board
# it, drive, steer and brake. Look for: the rover stands on its wheels (not sunk or floating),
# drives forward over the ground, turns right with D, stops with X; the log says "boarded the
# Rover".
0.5  press Z
1    wait landed 20
2    press F                 # step out
3    press G                 # a rover appears 8 m ahead
5    shot 01-rover.png
6    press F                 # board it
8    shot 02-in-rover.png
8.5  hold W 6                # drive forward
12   shot 03-driving.png
14.5 hold D 2                # turn right while still rolling
14.5 hold W 2
16   shot 04-turning.png
17   hold X 3                # brake
20   shot 05-stopped.png
21   quit
