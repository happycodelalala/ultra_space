# Walking on foot (task 2.9). Run with ULTRA_SPACE_START_ALTITUDE=1500: that is below the start
# spot's ground (~1920 m), so the ship is placed on the
# surface at once; Z first cancels its orbital speed so it rests instead of skidding and is landed.
# Step out, look around, run, jump, walk back and board.
0.5  press Z                # stop relative to the ground (the start keeps orbital speed)
1    wait landed 20          # the clock stops until the ship rests on the ground
2    press F                 # step out
4    shot 01-stepped-out.png
6    mouse 8 0 1             # turn right
8    shot 02-looking-around.png
9    mouse 0 -4 0.5          # look up a little
10   hold W 5                # run forward
10   hold Shift 5
13   shot 03-running.png
15.5 press Space             # jump
15.8 shot 04-jumping.png
17   mouse -16 0 1           # turn round
18.5 hold W 5
18.5 hold Shift 5
24   press F                 # board (back within reach after running back)
26   shot 05-boarded.png
27   quit
